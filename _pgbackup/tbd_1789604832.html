Hollywood: Beyond The Spotlight

Who owns a masterpiece when the artist is a machine? Hollywood stands at a critical intersection, caught between the promise of artificial intelligence and the human cost of its arrival. In high-stakes boardrooms across the Alliance of Motion Picture and Television Producers (AMPTP), studio executives face a defining question: how does an industry built on human imagination survive its own technological reinvention?

Delegates will step into the shoes of major studio executives as they navigate two core disruptions threatening the foundations of the silver screen. First, generative AI offers unprecedented speed and leaner budgets across visual effects, animation, and post-production. However, this efficiency demands a reckoning over worker displacement, massive energy costs from computing infrastructure, and unresolved copyright battles over training data. Second, sprawling global networks of outsourced VFX houses, streaming platforms, and international co-productions have left intellectual property vulnerable. A single leaked frame or stolen script can unravel millions in revenue overnight, leaving studios to decide who bears the financial blame when third-party security fails.

Delegates face a high-stakes mandate: leveraging cutting-edge technology without abandoning the creative workforce, while fortifying global production pipelines against piracy and betrayal. Delegates must collaborate to establish industry-wide security benchmarks, balance studio bottom lines with labor stability, and resolve complex intellectual property disputes. Profit, ethics, and innovation will collide in every negotiation. The question is not whether Hollywood will change, but who gets to decide how, and at what cost.
