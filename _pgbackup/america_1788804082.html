Co-Director

My name is Vedha Panchal, and I have the honour of serving as Under-Secretary-General of Crisis for TFSSMUN. The Cuban Missile Crisis first captured my attention through my studies in IB History, where I found myself less interested in the broad strokes of Cold War politics and more drawn to the thirteen days in October 1962 where the world sat at the brink of nuclear war. The decisions made in those rooms felt less like history and more like a masterclass in crisis leadership. That is what brought this committee to life. 

Outside of academics and MUN, I founded BlueKnot, an organization supporting neurodivergent individuals. I enjoy playing flag football, volleyball and tennis and have a passion for sketching, writing poetry, and dancing. I believe the best crisis committees do what good literature does: they put people in impossible situations and reveal something true about how we make decisions under pressure. Welcome to the room where it happened!
