Shaan Shah

Secretary-General

Shaan Shah serves as the President and Secretary-General of TFSSMUN III and is currently in his final year of the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme. His journey in Model United Nations began in Grade 10, and since then, MUN has become a major part of his high school experience. He has served as a Director for TFSSMUN since its inaugural iteration and currently continues his work in MUN as a Director for Markham Model United Nations. Beyond MUN, Shaan serves as an Ambassador for Queen’s University’s Leadership, Entrepreneurship, and Development Conferences and is actively involved in various childcare and community initiatives. Outside of his commitments, he enjoys swimming, dancing, playing badminton, and listening to Malcolm Todd and Kali Uchis. As he enters his third year with TFSSMUN, Shaan is incredibly excited to lead the Secretariat and bring TFSSMUN III to life. He hopes to make this year’s conference an experience that delegates, staff, and directors will remember long after the final gavel.
