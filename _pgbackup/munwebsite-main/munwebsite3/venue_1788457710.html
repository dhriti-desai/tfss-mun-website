Venue & Location

Information about our conference location, directions, and facilities.

Conference Location

TFSSMUN 2027 will take place at Turner Fenton Secondary School in Brampton, Ontario. Our facilities feature spacious committee rooms, opening and closing ceremony spaces, and designated areas for delegate networking and meals.

Venue Details & Logistics

Turner Fenton Secondary School is located at 7935 Kennedy Rd S, Brampton, ON L6X 0A8.
