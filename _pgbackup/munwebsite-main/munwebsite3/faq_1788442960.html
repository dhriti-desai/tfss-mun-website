About Turner Fenton MUN

TFSSMUN is a Model United Nations conference hosted in the Peel Region by high school students from Turner Fenton Secondary School. This year marks our third annual conference, bringing together delegates from across the region for diplomacy, debate, and collaboration. Our goal is to create an engaging environment where students can explore global issues, represent diverse perspectives, and develop their skills in public speaking, negotiation, and leadership.
